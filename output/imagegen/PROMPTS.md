# Fotografiile cazului / imagegen

Instrument: image_gen integrat, nu CLI. Personajele și fotografiile sunt inventate. PNG-urile originale sunt păstrate în acest folder; exporturile JPEG pentru web/PDF păstrează dimensiunile și compoziția.

## Prompt 1 — fotografii-caz.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Project asset: evidence photographs for a fictional offline Romanian escape room, Plicul dispărut. A precisely aligned 2 by 2 grid of four photographs, no borders, no captions, each quadrant same size. Natural mundane phone photography, not cinematic, Eastern European youth centre with white walls, folding tables, posters and daylight. All people fictional adults. Top left: 19-year-old woman Ana, dark shoulder-length hair, muted green sweatshirt, holding a cream envelope with handwritten Romanian text AFIȘE. Top right: preparation photo with U shaped tables, L shaped sofa on right, open door on left, temporary blue chair near doorway, Ana with navy blue folder; no money visible. Bottom left: cream envelope on grey table labelled 5 000 / atelier foto, blue tape repairing upper-right corner, counting slip headed 5 000. Bottom right: janitorial trolley with orange bucket and mop by an exterior wooden service door, no people or envelope. No elements crossing the grid, no police logos, guns, signatures, watermarks or overlaid UI.

## Prompt 2 — plic-recuperat.png

Use case: precise-object-edit. Reference: first contact sheet. A new single documentary photograph of the same cream envelope from its bottom-left panel. Preserve handwritten text 5 000 / atelier foto and blue tape on upper-right corner. Landscape, overhead slight oblique angle on an off-white desk, counting slip headed 5 000 with checked boxes, unbranded ruler along bottom edge. Same envelope, different photograph and desk. No people, police branding, seals, signatures, banknotes, extra text or collage. Natural evidence photography, sharp legible handwriting.

Operațiile ulterioare sunt export de format și încadrare în HTML/PDF. Nu s-au folosit fotografiile private ale centrului sau persoane reale ca personaje.
